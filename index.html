Mapping Kampung Muka

Mapping kampungs in Jakarta s an ongoing initiative to map buildings as part of an urban agrarian reform program that aims to establish collective land and/or building ownership through kampung cooperatives.

This survey is the second step of the community mapping process. After residents map their houses together, this survey collects the location and building information needed to create an accurate, scaled map.

The final map will support the documentation required by policymakers for collective ownership, providing residents with security of tenure while keeping kampung land and buildings outside the conventional property market.
